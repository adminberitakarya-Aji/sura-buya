# Review

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Review stage is the final quality control process before any content enters production.

Its purpose is to ensure that every generated artifact is consistent with the Universe Bible, aligns with the creator's vision, and is ready for production.

AI may generate content.

Only the creator approves it.

---

# Objective

Validate every production artifact before it becomes part of the official project.

The Review stage ensures:

- canon consistency
- character integrity
- narrative quality
- production readiness
- creative approval

No content proceeds to production without review.

---

# Review Workflow

```
Generated Content

↓

Automated Validation

↓

Review Package

↓

Creator Review

↓

Revision (if needed)

↓

Approval

↓

Production
```

The review process combines automated analysis with human judgment.

---

# Review Scope

Every generated artifact may be reviewed.

Examples include:

- Character Profiles
- World Information
- Story Plans
- Season Plans
- Episode Plans
- Scripts
- Dialogue
- Storyboards
- Visual References
- Production Assets

---

# Automated Validation

Before reaching the creator, the AI Engine performs automated checks.

Examples include:

- canon validation
- timeline validation
- relationship validation
- location validation
- character consistency
- naming consistency
- missing information
- structural completeness

The engine identifies potential issues but does not make creative decisions.

---

# Review Package

Instead of presenting raw AI output, the engine prepares a structured Review Package.

Example:

```
Episode 05

Status
✓ Generated Successfully

Canon
✓ Passed

Characters
✓ Consistent

Timeline
✓ Valid

Locations
✓ Valid

Dialogue

Warnings
1

Suggestions
2
```

The Review Package helps creators evaluate content efficiently.

---

# Creator Responsibilities

The creator evaluates whether the generated content:

- matches the intended vision
- respects the Universe Bible
- supports the story
- maintains quality
- is ready for production

The creator always has the final decision.

---

# Review Checklist

Creators should consider the following questions.

## Canon

- Does this contradict the Universe Bible?
- Are all established facts preserved?

---

## Characters

- Are personalities consistent?
- Are motivations believable?
- Do relationships remain accurate?
- Does dialogue fit each character?

---

## World

- Are locations correct?
- Are world rules respected?
- Is the environment consistent?

---

## Story

- Does the episode support the season?
- Does the season support the story?
- Is pacing appropriate?
- Are conflicts meaningful?

---

## Production

- Is the content production-ready?
- Are there missing assets?
- Are revisions required?

---

# Review Actions

The creator may choose one of the following actions.

## Approve

The artifact becomes approved for production.

---

## Edit

The creator manually modifies the content.

The edited version becomes the new working version.

---

## Request Revision

The creator asks the AI Engine to improve specific areas while preserving approved content.

Examples:

- improve dialogue
- improve pacing
- strengthen emotional impact
- simplify scenes

---

## Reject

The creator discards the generated artifact.

The engine may generate a completely new version if requested.

---

# Canon Updates

Sometimes the creator intentionally changes established canon.

Examples include:

- introducing new history
- changing relationships
- expanding world rules
- revising character backgrounds

These changes should update the appropriate Bible before production continues.

The Universe Bible always remains the Single Source of Truth.

---

# Review Outcomes

After review, every artifact receives a status.

Examples:

- Draft
- In Review
- Approved
- Revision Required
- Rejected
- Archived

The status determines whether production may continue.

---

# Design Principles

The Review stage follows these principles.

- Human Review
- Canon First
- Creator Authority
- Explainable Decisions
- Production Readiness

Quality is more important than generation speed.

---

# Relationship with the Engine

The Review stage marks the transition between AI generation and human approval.

```
AI Engine

↓

Validation

↓

Review Package

↓

Creator Decision

↓

Production
```

The engine assists.

The creator decides.

---

# Output

An approved artifact becomes an official production asset.

Depending on the workflow, outputs may include:

- Approved Character
- Approved World
- Approved Story
- Approved Season
- Approved Episode
- Approved Script
- Approved Storyboard
- Approved Visual Assets

Only approved artifacts may enter the Production stage.

---

# Summary

The Review stage ensures that every production artifact meets the project's standards before publication.

By combining automated validation with human judgment, the workflow preserves canon, maintains quality, and protects creative ownership.

The AI Engine accelerates production, but the creator always retains final authority over what becomes part of the Suro & Buya universe.
