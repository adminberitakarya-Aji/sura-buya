# Create World

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Create World stage defines the environment in which every story takes place.

A compelling world provides the rules, history, culture, and atmosphere that shape characters and influence every narrative decision.

Rather than serving as a simple backdrop, the world becomes an active participant in the story.

All approved world information becomes part of the World Bible.

---

# Objective

Build a coherent and believable world that supports long-term serialized storytelling.

The world should answer questions such as:

- Where does the story take place?
- How does society function?
- What rules govern the universe?
- What makes this world unique?
- What limitations exist?

---

# Creator Responsibilities

The creator defines the world according to the needs of the story.

The creator may begin with:

- a simple idea
- a location
- a culture
- a historical event
- a fantasy setting
- a futuristic city
- a complete world design

The AI Engine assists in expanding the world while preserving the creator's vision.

---

# World Components

A world may consist of multiple interconnected components.

---

## Geography

Define the physical environment.

Examples include:

- countries
- cities
- villages
- forests
- mountains
- oceans
- islands
- landmarks

Questions to consider:

- Where do characters live?
- What locations are important?
- How do people travel?

---

## History

Every world has a past.

Examples include:

- historical events
- wars
- discoveries
- disasters
- revolutions
- founding legends

History explains why the world exists in its current state.

---

## Society

Describe how people live together.

Examples:

- government
- laws
- economy
- education
- religion
- traditions
- family structures

Society influences character behavior and conflict.

---

## Culture

Culture gives the world its identity.

Examples:

- customs
- festivals
- languages
- cuisine
- clothing
- architecture
- art
- music

Culture should feel authentic and internally consistent.

---

## Technology

Define the technological level of the world.

Examples:

- traditional
- modern
- futuristic
- steampunk
- magical technology

Technology affects everyday life and storytelling possibilities.

---

## Rules of the World

Every universe has rules.

Examples include:

- physical laws
- magic systems
- spiritual systems
- scientific limitations
- supernatural rules

These rules should remain consistent throughout the series.

---

## Organizations

Organizations shape the world's structure.

Examples:

- governments
- schools
- guilds
- corporations
- military
- communities
- secret societies

Organizations often become important story elements.

---

## Important Locations

Identify recurring locations.

Examples:

- homes
- schools
- markets
- temples
- headquarters
- forests
- laboratories

Recurring locations strengthen audience familiarity.

---

## Timeline

Define major historical milestones.

Example:

```
Founding

↓

Golden Age

↓

Great Conflict

↓

Present Day
```

A timeline helps preserve continuity across seasons and episodes.

---

# AI Assistance

The AI Engine may assist by:

- expanding world descriptions
- suggesting cultures
- creating historical events
- proposing locations
- identifying inconsistencies
- improving internal logic
- enriching environmental details

The AI never changes established world rules without creator approval.

---

# Review Checklist

Before approval, creators should verify:

- Is the world internally consistent?
- Are the rules clearly defined?
- Can the world support multiple stories?
- Does the world encourage interesting conflicts?
- Are important locations identified?
- Is the history believable?
- Does the world match the intended tone?

---

# Output

Approved world information is stored in the World Bible.

The World Bible becomes the authoritative reference for:

- story planning
- episode generation
- location selection
- dialogue context
- visual development
- production planning

No production artifact should contradict the World Bible without updating the canonical source.

---

# Design Principles

World creation follows these principles:

- Canon First
- Context First
- Internal Consistency
- Long-Term Reusability
- Human Review

The world should remain stable while allowing room for future expansion.

---

# Future Revisions

Worlds naturally evolve.

Creators may later introduce:

- new locations
- historical discoveries
- political changes
- cultural developments
- technological advancements
- world-expanding events

All approved additions become canonical through updates to the World Bible.

---

# Summary

The world provides the foundation upon which every story unfolds.

The Create World stage transforms ideas into a structured and reusable World Bible that defines locations, history, culture, rules, and society.

Together with the Character Bible, the World Bible forms the core context that enables the AI Engine to generate consistent, believable, and scalable serialized stories.
