# Creator Workflow

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Creator Workflow defines the production journey from an initial idea to a production-ready serialized story.

It is designed to provide creators with a simple and intuitive experience while the AI Engine manages the complexity behind the scenes.

The workflow intentionally hides technical implementation details such as prompt engineering, context retrieval, orchestration, and validation.

Creators focus on storytelling.

The engine handles the production process.

---

# Workflow Overview

```
Create Character

        ▼

        ▼
Create Story
        │

Create Season
        │

Create Episode
        │
        ▼

        │

```

Each stage contributes new knowledge to the Universe Bible and prepares the next stage of production.

---

# Workflow Principles

The Creator Workflow follows several principles.

- Simple by design
- Guided rather than technical
- Progressive content creation
- Human review at every important milestone
- Canon preservation throughout production

Creators never need to understand how the AI Engine operates internally.

---

# Stage 1 — Create Character

## Objective

Introduce one or more characters that will become part of the universe.

Characters are the foundation of every story.

---

## Creator Responsibilities

The creator defines:

- character name
- personality
- appearance
- background
- motivation
- strengths
- weaknesses
- relationships

The creator may provide as much or as little information as desired.

The AI Engine assists in expanding incomplete ideas.

---

## Output

Character Bible

The generated character information becomes part of the Universe Bible.

---

# Stage 2 — Create World

## Objective

Define the environment where stories take place.

---

## Creator Responsibilities

Examples include:

- locations
- geography
- culture
- technology
- history
- social rules
- supernatural systems

The creator establishes the rules that govern the universe.

---

## Output

World Bible

---

# Stage 3 — Create Story

## Objective

Define the primary narrative of the series.

This stage establishes the long-term direction of the universe.

---

## Creator Responsibilities

Examples include:

- main conflict
- protagonist goals
- antagonist goals
- themes
- emotional tone
- ending direction

The creator determines what story the audience will experience.

---

## Output

Story Bible

---

# Stage 4 — Create Season

## Objective

Break the main story into seasons.

Each season should represent a meaningful chapter within the overall narrative.

---

## Creator Responsibilities

Examples include:

- season objective
- major conflict
- key events
- character development
- season ending

---

## Output

Season Plan

---

# Stage 5 — Create Episode

## Objective

Transform the season plan into individual episodes.

Each episode should contribute to the larger narrative while remaining engaging as a standalone experience.

---

## Creator Responsibilities

Examples include:

- episode objective
- important scenes
- emotional moments
- pacing
- cliffhangers

The engine assists with structure and continuity.

---

## Output

Episode Draft

---

# Stage 6 — Review

## Objective

Review every generated artifact before production.

Review ensures creative quality and canon consistency.

---

## Creator Responsibilities

The creator may:

- approve
- edit
- reject
- regenerate

Nothing enters production without approval.

---

## Review Focus

The creator should evaluate:

- character consistency
- world consistency
- story quality
- pacing
- dialogue
- emotional impact
- canon compliance

---

# Stage 7 — Production

## Objective

Convert approved content into production-ready assets.

Depending on the project, production may include:

- scripts
- storyboard
- illustrations
- voice assets
- animation assets
- publishing materials

Production consumes approved content only.

---

# Human and AI Responsibilities

## Creator

Responsible for:

- creative vision

- approvals
- canon changes

---

## AI Engine

Responsible for:

- retrieval

# Design Goals

The Creator Workflow is designed to achieve the following goals:

- Minimize manual work
- Eliminate prompt engineering
- Preserve creative ownership
- Maintain canon consistency
- Support long-running serialized stories
- Scale to multiple universes

---

# Summary

The Creator Workflow provides a structured yet flexible process for building serialized content.

Creators focus on ideas, characters, and stories.

The AI Engine manages orchestration, context, planning, and consistency.

Together, they transform a structured Universe Bible into production-ready serialized content while preserving creative ownership and narrative integrity.
