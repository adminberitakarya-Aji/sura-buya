# Create Character

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Create Character stage establishes the individuals who inhabit the universe.

Characters are the foundation of every serialized story.

Rather than designing plots first, the Suro & Buya AI Engine begins by building believable, consistent, and memorable characters.

Every future story, dialogue, relationship, and conflict originates from the characters defined in this stage.

---

# Objective

Create one or more characters that are:

- believable
- unique
- internally consistent
- capable of long-term development
- suitable for serialized storytelling

Each character becomes part of the Character Bible.

---

# Creator Responsibilities

The creator provides the creative vision for the character.

The amount of information may vary.

The creator may provide:

- a complete character profile
- a rough idea
- only a name
- only a personality
- only an illustration

The AI Engine assists in expanding incomplete information while preserving the creator's intent.

---

# Minimum Information

A character should include at least:

- Name
- Role
- Personality
- Description

Example:

```
Name:
Buya

Role:
Mentor

Personality:
Wise, calm, humorous

Description:
An elderly storyteller who guides younger generations through wisdom and kindness.
```

---

# Recommended Information

For richer storytelling, additional information is encouraged.

## Identity

- Full name
- Nickname
- Age
- Gender
- Occupation
- Species
- Nationality

---

## Appearance

Examples:

- hairstyle
- clothing
- body type
- facial features
- accessories
- colors
- visual identity

---

## Personality

Examples:

- strengths
- weaknesses
- habits
- fears
- motivations
- values
- emotional traits

---

## Background

Examples:

- childhood
- education
- family
- important life events
- personal history

---

## Relationships

Examples:

- family
- friends
- rivals
- enemies
- mentors
- students

Relationships should reference existing characters whenever possible.

---

## Abilities

Examples:

- skills
- talents
- powers
- knowledge
- limitations

Abilities should remain consistent with the World Bible.

---

## Goals

Examples:

- short-term goals
- long-term goals
- dreams
- ambitions
- responsibilities

Goals drive future stories.

---

## Character Arc

The creator may define how the character evolves over time.

Examples:

- growth
- redemption
- corruption
- maturity
- sacrifice

Character development should occur gradually across episodes and seasons.

---

# AI Assistance

The AI Engine may assist by:

- expanding personality
- suggesting motivations
- identifying inconsistencies
- proposing relationships
- improving descriptions
- generating visual references
- generating dialogue samples

The AI never changes established facts without creator approval.

---

# Review Checklist

Before approval, creators should verify:

- Is the personality believable?
- Is the motivation clear?
- Are strengths and weaknesses balanced?
- Is the character visually distinctive?
- Does the character fit the universe?
- Does the character have room to grow?
- Is the character different from existing characters?

---

# Output

Approved characters are stored in the Character Bible.

The Character Bible becomes the authoritative reference for:

- Story planning
- Episode generation
- Dialogue generation
- Visual generation
- Production assets

No production artifact should redefine an approved character without updating the Character Bible.

---

# Design Principles

Character creation follows these principles:

- Character First
- Canon First
- Human Review
- Context First
- Consistency Over Novelty

These principles ensure that characters remain recognizable throughout the entire series.

---

# Future Revisions

Characters are living entities within the universe.

As the story evolves, creators may:

- update personalities
- add relationships
- introduce new abilities
- expand backgrounds
- define new story arcs

All approved revisions become part of the Character Bible and serve as the new canonical reference.

---

# Summary

Characters are the heart of every serialized story.

The Create Character stage transforms creative ideas into structured, reusable knowledge stored within the Character Bible.

This structured foundation enables the AI Engine to generate stories, dialogue, and future productions that remain faithful to each character's identity, personality, and long-term development.
