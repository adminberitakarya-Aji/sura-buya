# Create Episode

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Create Episode stage transforms a Season Plan into an individual production-ready episode.

An episode is the smallest narrative unit within a serialized production.

While each episode should deliver a satisfying experience on its own, it must also contribute to the progression of the season and the overall story.

Every episode is generated from the Universe Bible and the approved Season Plan.

---

# Objective

Design an episode that:

- advances the season narrative
- develops the characters
- respects established canon
- delivers meaningful emotional moments
- prepares the audience for the next episode

An episode should never exist in isolation.

It is one chapter within a larger serialized story.

---

# Creator Responsibilities

The creator defines the direction of the episode.

The creator may provide:

- episode objective
- major events
- important scenes
- emotional moments
- dialogue ideas
- cliffhanger concepts

The AI Engine assists by expanding these ideas into a structured episode.

# Episode Components

Every episode consists of several structured elements.

---

## Episode Metadata

Basic production information.

Examples:

- Episode Number
- Episode Title
- Runtime
- Season Number
- Status

---

## Episode Summary

Provide a short overview of the episode.

The summary should explain:

- what happens
- why it matters
- how it affects the season

---

## Episode Objective

Define the primary purpose of the episode.

Examples:

- introduce a new character
- reveal important information
- strengthen relationships
- increase tension
- resolve a previous conflict

Every episode should have one clear objective.

---

## Story Beats

Identify the major narrative moments.

Example:

```
Opening

↓

Inciting Event

↓

Escalation

↓

Turning Point

↓

Climax

↓

Ending
```

These beats provide the backbone of the episode.

---

## Scene Planning

Break the episode into individual scenes.

Each scene should define:

- location
- participating characters
- objective
- emotional tone
- expected outcome

Every scene should move the story forward.

---

## Character Participation

Identify which characters appear.

For each character define:

- purpose
- emotional state
- relationship changes
- contribution to the story

Characters should never appear without narrative purpose.

---

## Dialogue Direction

Dialogue should reflect:

- personality
- relationships
- current emotional state
- story context

Dialogue should support character development rather than simply deliver information.

---

## Emotional Progression

Define the emotional flow of the episode.

Example:

```
Curiosity

↓

Hope

↓

Conflict

↓

Fear

↓

Relief
```

Emotional progression keeps the audience engaged.

---

## Cliffhanger (Optional)

If the episode continues into another story segment, define how it ends.

Examples:

- unanswered question
- unexpected reveal
- new conflict
- shocking discovery

A cliffhanger should encourage viewers to continue without feeling manipulative.

---

# AI Assistance

The AI Engine may assist by:

- expanding story beats
- planning scenes
- generating dialogue
- improving pacing
- identifying continuity issues
- balancing emotional progression
- suggesting stronger endings

The AI operates within the constraints of the Universe Bible and Season Plan.

---

# Review Checklist

Before approval, creators should verify:

- Does the episode achieve its objective?
- Does every scene have a purpose?
- Are characters consistent?
- Does dialogue sound natural?
- Does the pacing feel balanced?
- Does the episode support the season?
- Does it respect the established canon?
- Does it leave the audience wanting more?

---

# Output

Approved episode information becomes part of the Production Bible.

The Episode Plan serves as the primary reference for:

- script writing
- storyboard creation
- voice production
- visual production
- animation
- publishing

Only approved episodes proceed to production.

# Design Principles

Episode planning follows these principles:

- Canon First
- Character First
- Story First
- Context First
- Emotional Continuity
- Human Review

Episodes should strengthen both the immediate narrative and the long-term series.

---

# Future Revisions

Episodes may evolve during production.

Creators may:

- rewrite scenes
- adjust pacing
- modify dialogue
- introduce additional moments
- improve emotional impact
- refine the ending

All approved changes should update the Episode Plan before production begins.

---

# Summary

The Create Episode stage transforms a Season Plan into a structured, production-ready episode.

Each episode combines narrative structure, character development, scene planning, dialogue direction, and emotional progression into a cohesive blueprint.

Together with the Universe Bible and Season Plan, the Episode Plan enables the AI Engine to generate consistent, engaging, and production-ready serialized content while preserving canon and creative intent.
