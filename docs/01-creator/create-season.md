# Create Season

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Create Season stage transforms the long-term narrative defined in the Story Bible into manageable story arcs.

Each season represents a significant chapter within the overall story, containing its own beginning, progression, climax, and resolution while contributing to the larger narrative.

The goal is to ensure every season feels complete on its own while advancing the series toward its ultimate conclusion.

---

# Objective

Design a season that:

- advances the main story
- develops the characters
- introduces meaningful conflicts
- maintains narrative continuity
- prepares future seasons

Every approved season becomes part of the Story Bible and Production Bible.

---

# Creator Responsibilities

The creator defines the creative direction for the season.

The creator may provide:

- a season concept
- a short synopsis
- major events
- character goals
- ending ideas
- episode count

The AI Engine assists in organizing these ideas into a structured season plan.

# Season Components

Each season consists of several structured elements.

---

## Season Overview

Provide a short summary of the season.

Example:

> Suro and Buya travel across several villages, helping communities overcome conflicts while uncovering a larger mystery that connects every encounter.

The overview establishes the identity of the season.

---

## Season Objective

Define what the season intends to accomplish.

Examples:

- introduce the main world
- develop important relationships
- reveal hidden truths
- begin a larger conflict
- conclude a major storyline

Every season should have a clear objective.

---

## Character Development

Identify how each major character changes during the season.

Examples:

- new friendships
- increased confidence
- emotional healing
- growing responsibilities
- personal sacrifice

Character development should feel gradual and meaningful.

---

## Season Conflict

Describe the primary conflict that drives the season.

Examples:

- a dangerous rival
- political instability
- personal struggles
- natural disasters
- moral dilemmas

The conflict should remain relevant throughout the season.

---

## Major Events

Identify important story milestones.

Examples:

- meeting a new ally
- discovering an ancient secret
- losing an important character
- defeating a major enemy
- revealing hidden motivations

These events become anchor points for episode planning.

---

## Episode Structure

Define the approximate number of episodes.

Example:

```
Episode 1

↓

Episode 2

↓

Episode 3

↓

...

↓

Final Episode
```

The AI Engine may later refine this structure.

---

## Season Climax

Define the emotional and narrative peak of the season.

The climax should resolve the season's primary conflict while opening opportunities for future stories.

---

## Ending

Determine how the season concludes.

Possible endings include:

- complete resolution
- cliffhanger
- emotional closure
- transition to the next season

The ending should encourage continued engagement.

---

# AI Assistance

The AI Engine may assist by:

- organizing season structure
- balancing pacing
- identifying missing story beats
- suggesting episode distribution
- improving character progression
- ensuring consistency with previous seasons
- proposing stronger climaxes

The AI supports planning without replacing creative decisions.

---

# Review Checklist

Before approval, creators should verify:

- Does the season support the Story Bible?
- Is the pacing appropriate?
- Does every major character develop?
- Is the conflict engaging?
- Does the climax feel earned?
- Is the ending satisfying?
- Does the season naturally lead into the next stage of the story?

---

# Output

Approved season information becomes part of the Story Bible and Production Bible.

The Season Plan becomes the primary reference for:

- episode planning
- script generation
- production scheduling
- visual planning
- marketing preparation

No episode should contradict the approved Season Plan.

# Design Principles

Season planning follows these principles:

- Story First
- Character First
- Canon First
- Context First
- Progressive Development
- Human Review

Each season should feel like a complete chapter while remaining part of a larger serialized narrative.

---

# Future Revisions

Season plans may evolve before production begins.

Creators may:

- add episodes
- remove episodes
- revise pacing
- introduce new characters
- modify conflicts
- expand story arcs

Any approved revision updates the canonical Season Plan before episode production begins.

---

# Summary

The Create Season stage transforms the Story Bible into a structured production roadmap.

Each season organizes the long-term narrative into a coherent arc with clear objectives, meaningful character development, major events, and a satisfying conclusion.

The approved Season Plan provides the foundation for episode planning while ensuring every episode contributes to the broader vision of the series.
