# Story Bible

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Story Bible is the authoritative repository that defines the narrative foundation of the Suro & Buya universe.

It establishes what stories exist, how they are structured, how they evolve over time, and how every narrative connects to the larger universe.

The Story Bible ensures that every season, episode, scene, and dialogue contributes to a coherent and consistent narrative.

---

# Objective

Provide a complete and canonical definition of the narrative structure of the universe.

The Story Bible answers one fundamental question:

> **"What happens in this universe?"**

Every AI-generated story should originate from and remain consistent with the Story Bible.

# Story Premise

The premise defines the core idea of the universe.

It explains:

- what the story is about
- why the story matters
- the central narrative direction
- the intended audience

The premise should remain stable throughout the lifetime of the series.

---

# Themes

Themes define the core messages of the universe.

Examples include:

- friendship
- family
- courage
- responsibility
- honesty
- compassion
- perseverance

Themes guide creative decisions across every episode.

---

# Narrative Structure

Defines how stories are organized.

Examples:

- episodic storytelling
- serialized storytelling
- multi-season arcs
- standalone episodes
- recurring narrative patterns

The chosen structure should remain consistent.

---

# Story Arcs

Story arcs describe long-term narrative progression.

Examples:

- character growth
- major conflicts
- long-term mysteries
- emotional journeys
- season objectives

Story arcs connect individual episodes into a larger narrative.

---

# Timeline

The timeline defines the chronological order of events.

It records:

- major historical events
- season progression
- episode order
- important milestones
- character development events

The timeline ensures narrative continuity.

---

# Major Events

Defines significant events that shape the universe.

Examples:

- important discoveries
- festivals
- disasters
- celebrations
- turning points
- life-changing decisions

Major events become canonical references for future stories.

---

# Conflicts

Defines recurring sources of narrative tension.

Examples:

- internal conflict
- interpersonal conflict
- environmental challenges
- community problems
- moral dilemmas

Conflicts should support the themes rather than exist for their own sake.

---

# Season Structure

Each season should have a defined purpose.

Examples include:

- season objective
- opening situation
- midpoint
- climax
- resolution

Every episode should contribute to the season objective.

---

# Episode Structure

Each episode should include:

- episode objective
- central conflict
- key events
- emotional progression
- resolution
- narrative outcome

Episodes should function independently while advancing the larger story.

---

# Continuity

Continuity preserves consistency across the series.

Checks include:

- timeline consistency
- character progression
- unresolved storylines
- recurring locations
- recurring themes
- long-term consequences

Every new story should acknowledge established canon.

---

# Story Metadata

Every story element should include metadata.

Examples:

- story ID
- season number
- episode number
- version
- status
- approval date

Metadata supports retrieval and version control.

# Change Management

Narrative changes must be carefully managed.

Examples include:

- new story arcs
- episode revisions
- season restructuring
- timeline updates
- conflict revisions

Every change should:

- be reviewed
- be approved
- receive a new version
- include change history

Story continuity should never be broken without explicit approval.

# Summary

The Story Bible is the canonical definition of every narrative within the Suro & Buya universe.
