# Glossary

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Glossary defines the official terminology used throughout the Suro & Buya documentation.

Its purpose is to ensure that creators, engineers, reviewers, and future contributors use a shared vocabulary when discussing the AI Engine, the production workflow, and the Universe Bible.

Every important term should have one official definition.

---

# Usage

The definitions in this document apply to all documentation unless explicitly stated otherwise.

When multiple documents use the same term, this glossary serves as the authoritative reference.

---

# A

## AI Engine

The complete system responsible for transforming creator intent into production-ready artifacts through structured workflows.

The AI Engine includes planning, generation, validation, and review components.

---

## Artifact

Any document, asset, or output produced during the creative or production process.

Examples include:

- Character Profile
- Story Plan
- Episode Plan
- Script
- Storyboard
- Visual Asset
- Voice Asset
- Production Package

---

## Asset

A production-ready resource used to create the final content.

Examples include:

- illustrations
- audio
- subtitles
- thumbnails
- storyboards

---

# B

## Bible Retrieval

The engine component responsible for retrieving relevant information from the Universe Bible.

Only the information required for the current task is retrieved.

---

## Canon

The officially approved facts that define the Suro & Buya universe.

Canon includes characters, locations, events, rules, visual identity, and production standards.

---

## Canon Validator

The engine component responsible for validating that the Working Context does not contradict the Universe Bible before content generation begins.

---

## Character Bible

The canonical repository describing every character in the universe.

---

## Context Builder

The engine component responsible for assembling all retrieved information into a Working Context.

---

## Creator

The human responsible for making creative decisions and approving generated content.

The creator always has final authority.

---

## Creator Workflow

The structured process followed by creators from idea creation to production approval.

---

# D

## Dialogue Generator

The engine component responsible for generating dialogue that aligns with the approved plans and Character Bible.

---

## Draft

An artifact that has not yet been approved.

Drafts should never be treated as canon.

---

# E

## Engine Workflow

The internal workflow followed by the AI Engine to transform creator requests into production-ready artifacts.

---

## Episode

A single narrative unit within a season.

Episodes are created from approved Episode Plans.

---

## Episode Planner

The engine component responsible for transforming a Season Plan into a structured Episode Plan.

---

# G

## Generation

The process of creating new content using the AI Engine.

Generation always follows planning and validation.

---

# H

## Human Review

The stage where creators inspect AI-generated content before approval.

Human Review is mandatory before production.

---

# I

## Intent Detection

The engine component responsible for identifying the creator's request and determining the appropriate workflow.

---

# P

## Production

The workflow that transforms approved creative artifacts into publishable assets.

---

## Production Bible

The canonical repository describing production standards, workflows, naming conventions, metadata, and publishing rules.

---

## Production Package

A collection of validated production assets prepared for publishing.

---

## Publishing

The final stage that releases approved production packages.

---

# Q

## Quality Assurance (QA)

The process of verifying that production assets satisfy creative and technical standards before publishing.

---

# R

## Review Package

A structured document containing generated artifacts, validation reports, recommendations, and metadata for creator review.

---

# S

## Scene

A narrative unit within an episode.

Scenes consist of one or more shots during production.

---

## Scene Generator

The engine component responsible for transforming an Episode Plan into detailed scene descriptions.

---

## Season

A collection of related episodes forming a larger narrative arc.

---

## Season Planner

The engine component responsible for transforming a Story Plan into a Season Plan.

---

## Single Source of Truth

The principle that the Universe Bible is the only authoritative source of canonical information.

---

## Story Bible

The canonical repository describing narrative structure, themes, timeline, story arcs, seasons, and episodes.

---

## Story Planner

The engine component responsible for transforming a Story Bible into a structured Story Plan.

---

## Storyboard

A visual representation of the approved production script organized into scenes and shots.

---

# U

## Universe Bible

The highest-level repository containing all canonical knowledge about the Suro & Buya universe.

It consists of:

- Character Bible
- World Bible
- Story Bible
- Visual Bible
- Production Bible

---

# V

## Versioning

The process of managing changes to documents, assets, and published releases through structured version control.

---

## Visual Bible

The canonical repository defining the visual identity of the universe.

---

## Visual Production

The workflow responsible for creating production-ready visual assets from approved storyboards.

---

# W

## Working Context

A temporary collection of relevant information assembled by the AI Engine for a specific task.

The Working Context exists only during execution and is not stored as permanent canon.

---

## World Bible

The canonical repository describing geography, locations, culture, history, organizations, environment, and world rules.

---

# Design Principles

The Glossary follows these principles:

- One Term, One Meaning
- Consistent Vocabulary
- Canonical Definitions
- Shared Understanding
- Easy Reference

Every important concept should have exactly one official definition.

---

# Maintenance

When introducing a new concept into the documentation:

1. Define the term.
2. Add it to this Glossary.
3. Use the glossary definition consistently throughout all documents.
4. Update the glossary version if new official terminology is introduced.

The Glossary should evolve together with the project.

---

# Summary

The Glossary is the official dictionary of the Suro & Buya project.

By defining consistent terminology for creators, engineers, reviewers, and AI systems, it establishes a shared language that improves communication, reduces ambiguity, and strengthens the documentation as the project grows.

Every important concept should have one official definition, and every document should use that definition consistently.
