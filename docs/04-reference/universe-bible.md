# Universe Bible

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Universe Bible is the highest-level knowledge repository of the Suro & Buya universe.

It defines the canonical truth shared by every creator, every production process, and every AI component.

Everything produced within the Suro & Buya ecosystem must originate from, align with, and remain consistent with the Universe Bible.

It is the **Single Source of Truth** for the entire project.

---

# Objective

Provide a centralized and authoritative foundation that preserves the consistency, continuity, and identity of the Suro & Buya universe.

The Universe Bible answers one fundamental question:

> **"What is considered canon in this universe?"**

Anything outside the Universe Bible is considered non-canonical until officially approved.

---

# Role in the Ecosystem

The Universe Bible is used by every workflow.

```
Universe Bible

↓

Creator Workflow

↓

Engine Workflow

↓

Production Workflow

↓

Published Content
```

Every stage depends on the Universe Bible.

---

# Composition

The Universe Bible is composed of several specialized Bibles.

```
Universe Bible

├── Character Bible
├── World Bible
├── Story Bible
├── Visual Bible
└── Production Bible
```

Each Bible has a single responsibility.

Together they define the complete universe.

---

# Character Bible

Defines every character within the universe.

Includes:

- identity
- biography
- personality
- motivations
- relationships
- abilities
- weaknesses
- character arc
- dialogue style

The Character Bible answers:

> **"Who are the characters?"**

---

# World Bible

Defines the world in which the story takes place.

Includes:

- geography
- locations
- history
- culture
- organizations
- technology
- social rules
- environmental rules

The World Bible answers:

> **"Where does the story happen?"**

---

# Story Bible

Defines the narrative structure of the universe.

Includes:

- premise
- themes
- chronology
- timeline
- major events
- conflicts
- story arcs
- seasons

The Story Bible answers:

> **"What happens in this universe?"**

---

# Visual Bible

Defines the visual identity of the series.

Includes:

- art direction
- color language
- character appearance
- environments
- props
- costumes
- visual motifs
- composition guidelines

The Visual Bible answers:

> **"How should the universe look?"**

---

# Production Bible

Defines production standards.

Includes:

- workflow
- naming conventions
- asset standards
- file organization
- metadata
- versioning
- publishing requirements

The Production Bible answers:

> **"How should the universe be produced?"**

---

# Canon

Everything inside the Universe Bible is considered canon.

Canon includes:

- characters
- locations
- events
- history
- visual identity
- production standards

Every generated artifact must respect canon.

---

# Single Source of Truth

The Universe Bible is the only authoritative reference.

```
Creator

↓

Universe Bible

↓

Engine

↓

Production
```

No component should maintain an independent version of canonical information.

---

# Engine Usage

During content generation, the AI Engine retrieves only the information required for the current task.

Example:

```
Creator Request

↓

Bible Retrieval

↓

Relevant Bible Sections

↓

Working Context

↓

Generation
```

The engine does not load the entire Universe Bible unless necessary.

---

# Change Management

The Universe Bible evolves through controlled revisions.

Every change should:

- be reviewed
- be approved
- receive a version
- include change history

Canon should never change silently.

---

# Versioning

Every Bible maintains its own version while remaining synchronized with the Universe Bible.

Example:

```
Universe Bible

v1.2

├── Character Bible v1.2
├── World Bible v1.2
├── Story Bible v1.2
├── Visual Bible v1.2
└── Production Bible v1.2
```

Version consistency ensures reliable production.

---

# Relationships

The Bibles are interconnected.

```
Character Bible

↓

Story Bible

↓

World Bible

↓

Visual Bible

↓

```

A change in one Bible may affect others.

Dependencies should always be reviewed before approval.

The Universe Bible is the foundation of the Suro & Buya ecosystem.

Every story begins with the Universe Bible.
