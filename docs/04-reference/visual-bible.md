# Visual Bible

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Visual Bible is the authoritative repository that defines the visual identity of the Suro & Buya universe.

It establishes how every character, location, object, environment, and visual element should appear across every illustration, storyboard, animation, promotional asset, and future production.

The Visual Bible ensures that the universe maintains a recognizable and consistent visual language regardless of who or what creates the artwork.

---

# Objective

Provide a complete and canonical definition of the visual identity of the universe.

The Visual Bible answers one fundamental question:

> **"How should this universe look?"**

Every visual asset produced by creators, artists, or AI must follow this document.
