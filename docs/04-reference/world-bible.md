# World Bible

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The World Bible is the authoritative repository that defines the world of the Suro & Buya universe.

It describes the physical environment, history, society, culture, rules, and systems that shape every story.

The World Bible ensures that every location, event, and interaction takes place within a consistent and believable universe.

---

# Objective

Provide a complete and canonical definition of the world in which every story takes place.

The World Bible answers one fundamental question:

> **"Where does the story happen?"**

Every AI-generated scene, event, and location should be grounded in this document.
