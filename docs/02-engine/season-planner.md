# Season Planner

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Season Planner transforms an approved Story Plan into one or more structured seasons.

Instead of writing episodes immediately, the Season Planner first divides the long-term narrative into meaningful production units.

Each season should function as a complete narrative chapter while contributing to the overall story.

---

# Objective

Create a structured Season Plan that organizes the Story Plan into manageable production units.

The Season Planner answers one fundamental question:

> **"How should the story be divided into seasons?"**

A well-designed season should have its own beginning, progression, climax, and conclusion while advancing the larger narrative.

# Responsibilities

The Season Planner is responsible for:

- dividing the Story Plan into seasons
- defining season objectives
- organizing major story arcs
- distributing character development
- balancing narrative pacing
- preparing Season Plans

The Season Planner does **not** generate episodes or scripts.

Its responsibility is planning.

# Season Components

Each Season Plan contains several planning components.

---

## Season Goal

Define what the season should accomplish.

Examples:

- introduce the universe
- reveal the main conflict
- develop important relationships
- prepare the final confrontation
- resolve a major mystery

Every season should have one primary objective.

---

## Season Summary

Provide a concise overview of the season.

The summary explains:

- what happens
- why it matters
- how it advances the story

---

## Story Arc

Identify the portion of the Story Plan covered by the season.

Example:

```
Season 1

Introduction

↓

Discovery

↓

First Turning Point
```

Every season represents one meaningful segment of the overall narrative.

---

## Major Events

Identify the most important events of the season.

Examples:

- discovering a hidden truth
- meeting a new ally
- defeating an antagonist
- losing an important character
- revealing a secret

These events become anchors for Episode Planning.

---

## Character Development

Define how major characters evolve during the season.

Examples:

- increased confidence
- strengthened friendships
- emotional healing
- growing leadership
- changing beliefs

Character growth should occur gradually across episodes.

---

## Season Conflict

Define the primary conflict of the season.

Examples:

- external threat
- personal struggle
- political conflict
- moral dilemma
- environmental challenge

The conflict should escalate naturally throughout the season.

---

## Season Climax

Identify the emotional and narrative peak.

The climax should:

- resolve the season conflict
- reward previous story development
- create lasting impact

---

## Season Ending

Determine how the season concludes.

Possible endings include:

- complete resolution
- cliffhanger
- transition
- new mystery
- preparation for the next season

The ending should motivate continued engagement.

---

```
Season 3

Escalation

↓

```

---

# Planning Principles

The Season Planner follows these principles.

## Story First

Every season serves the larger story.

---

## Character First

Character growth should continue naturally across seasons.

---

## Balanced Pacing

Avoid placing all major events in one season.

Important moments should be distributed evenly.

---

## Progressive Escalation

Each season should increase narrative stakes.

The audience should feel that the story is moving forward.

---

## Meaningful Resolution

Each season should provide a satisfying conclusion while encouraging continued viewing.

---

# Validation

Before approval, the planner verifies:

- alignment with the Story Plan
- balanced pacing
- consistent character development
- logical conflict progression
- sustainable future seasons
- canon consistency

Only validated Season Plans continue to Episode Planning.

# Summary

The Season Planner transforms a long-term Story Plan into structured, production-ready seasons.

By organizing narrative progression, character development, conflicts, and major events into meaningful seasonal arcs, the planner provides the foundation for episode planning while ensuring consistency, pacing, and long-term storytelling across the entire series.
