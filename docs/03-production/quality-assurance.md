# Quality Assurance

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

Quality Assurance (QA) is the final verification process before production assets are approved for publishing.

While the AI Engine validates planning and generation, Quality Assurance validates the complete production package to ensure that every asset is accurate, complete, consistent, and ready for release.

QA protects the integrity of the Suro & Buya universe by preventing production errors from reaching the audience.

---

# Objective

Verify that every production asset satisfies creative, technical, and production standards.

Quality Assurance answers one fundamental question:

> **"Is this production package ready to be published?"**

Only approved production packages may proceed to Publishing.

# Responsibilities

Quality Assurance is responsible for:

- verifying production completeness
- validating canon consistency
- validating asset quality
- detecting production errors
- preparing approval reports
- recommending revisions when necessary

Quality Assurance does **not** create or modify assets.

Its responsibility is verification.

# QA Categories

Quality Assurance verifies multiple production areas.

---

## Script Verification

Verify that the production script is complete.

Checks include:

- scene completeness
- dialogue completeness
- episode metadata
- production notes
- revision status

The production script should remain identical to the approved version.

---

## Storyboard Verification

Verify storyboard quality.

Checks include:

- complete scene coverage
- shot sequence
- visual continuity
- storyboard completeness

The storyboard should accurately represent the production script.

---

## Visual Verification

Verify all visual assets.

Checks include:

- character appearance
- locations
- props
- colors
- visual consistency
- missing assets

Every visual should follow the Visual Bible.

---

## Voice Verification

Verify all voice assets.

Checks include:

- dialogue completeness
- speaker assignment
- emotional consistency
- narration
- synchronization references

Voice assets should accurately represent the approved script.

---

## Canon Verification

Verify consistency with the Universe Bible.

Checks include:

- characters
- locations
- story events
- relationships
- timeline
- visual identity

No production asset should contradict established canon.

---

## Metadata Verification

Verify production metadata.

Checks include:

- identifiers
- naming conventions
- versions
- production status
- asset references

Metadata should remain complete and consistent.

# Severity Levels

Detected issues are classified according to severity.

---

## Information

General observation.

No action required.

---

## Warning

Minor issue.

Publishing may continue after creator review.

---

## Error

Production issue requiring correction.

Publishing should be postponed until resolved.

---

## Critical

Major issue affecting story integrity or production quality.

Publishing is prohibited.

# Revision Workflow

If issues are detected, only affected assets should be revised.

Example:

```
Voice Issue

↓

Voice Production

↓

Voice Validation

↓

Quality Assurance

↓

Approved
```

Targeted revisions reduce production time while preserving approved assets.

# Summary

Quality Assurance is the final production safeguard of the Suro & Buya engine.

By validating scripts, storyboards, visual assets, voice assets, metadata, and canon consistency before publishing, QA ensures that every production package meets the project's creative and technical standards.

The purpose of Quality Assurance is not to create content.

Its purpose is to ensure that every published episode faithfully represents the approved Universe Bible and is ready for audiences.
